AI learning paths
AI Learning Paths
High School
What can AI do and how do I use it?
1. What is AI / History — narrow vs general AI, key milestones, AI winters
2. How ML Works — training on data, supervised vs unsupervised, bias in = bias out
3. Neural Networks — neurons, layers, weights, what training means
4. NLP — tokens, embeddings, context windows, why LLMs predict tokens
5. Prompt Engineering — role, task, context, constraints, why examples matter
6. Ethics & Society — bias, deepfakes, job displacement, privacy, IP
7. Applications — vision, speech, recommendations; where AI fails and why
8. Hands-on Project — build a classifier or chatbot, evaluate it
College
How does it work, why does it fail, how do I build it?
Math Foundation — linear algebra, calculus, probability (prerequisites)
ML as a Discipline — bias-variance, overfitting, SVMs, decision trees, k-means
Deep Learning — CNNs, RNNs, Transformers, transfer learning, fine-tuning
NLP Deep Dive — attention, transformer architecture, "Attention is All You Need"
Systems & Engineering — GPUs, MLOps, RAG, vector databases, embeddings
Ethics (Formal) — frameworks, EU AI Act, NIST, case law
Research Literacy — reading papers, reproducibility, running experiments
Specialisations — computer vision, RL, AI safety, multimodal models
Adult Bootcamp
What will get me hired in 8–12 weeks?
Wk 1–2: Tooling — Python, pandas, Jupyter, Git, first API call day one
Wk 3–4: Prompt Engineering — RTCC, XML, few-shot, building a chatbot/classifier
Wk 5–6: RAG — embeddings, vector databases, document Q&A system
Wk 7–8: Agents — tool use, multi-step loops, LangChain, real agent project
Wk 9–10: Data & Fine-tuning — when to fine-tune vs prompt, training data prep
Wk 11–12: Production — deploy with Flask/FastAPI, cost management, portfolio

Gap: can build with AI but can't debug unexpected failures without the theory.
Practitioner-Leader — your path
Leading a team building an internal agent
1. Agent Architecture — tools, memory, planning, loops; 7 skills framework; failure modes
2. Prompt Engineering — RTCC + XML, few-shot, chain of thought, systematic evaluation
3. RAG & Knowledge Design — when agent needs knowledge base vs context injection
4. Evaluation & Observability — evals, test cases, logging, cost at scale
5. Human-in-the-Loop — approval gates, confidence signalling, escalation paths
6. Leading an AI Team — Scrum changes, who owns prompts, who owns evals, AI-DLC
7. Internal Agent Specifics — permissions, compliance, audit trails, change management
8. Meta-AI — AI to optimise prompts, write evals, run retros, improve the process

Priority order: 1. Evaluation design  2. RAG  3. Human-in-the-loop  4. Leading an AI team
Skip: backprop math, training from scratch, deep learning internals, MLOps
